**pkuinfo** builds open-source tools for campus life at Peking University. One login, every campus
service, and an assistant that can act for you. Your credentials stay on your device.

pkuinfo 是一群北大同学做的开源项目：一次登录，打通课表、校园卡、网费、树洞、教学网等校园服务，
再交给一个懂你的 AI 助手。凭据只保存在你自己的设备上。

### Projects

| | |
|---|---|
| [**pku-harness**](https://github.com/pkuinfo/pku-harness) | AI-native campus app (Android first, iOS next). The app is a harness; every capability is a plugin. |
| [**pku-harness-plugins**](https://github.com/pkuinfo/pku-harness-plugins) | Plugin SDK, all plugins (built-in and community, all equal), and the marketplace registry. |
| [**pkucli**](https://github.com/pkuinfo/pkucli) | Command-line tools and agent skills for PKU services: treehole, Blackboard, campus card, elective, finance, room booking… |
| [**pkubdcard**](https://github.com/pkuinfo/pkubdcard) | Share your campus-card payment code safely, with time limits and verification. |
| [**awesome-pkuinfo**](https://github.com/pkuinfo/awesome-pkuinfo) | A curated list of PKU-related open-source projects. |

### Principles

- **Your credentials, your device.** Campus passwords never leave your phone or your keyring.
- **Everything is a plugin.** We build the harness; the community builds the content.
- **Human and AI are equal operators.** Anything you can do by tap, the assistant can do by tool, and vice versa.

### Contribute

Write a plugin for a campus service you use: start with
[pku-harness-plugins](https://github.com/pkuinfo/pku-harness-plugins). Protocols for PKU services
live in [pkucli](https://github.com/pkuinfo/pkucli). Issues and pull requests are welcome in every repo.
